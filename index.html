🔧 Y/S Plan Fault Finder

Step-by-step diagnosis for UK domestic heating controls — mid-position (Y plan) and 2-port zone valve (S plan) systems.

Safety first: isolate before touching wiring. Prove dead with an approved two-pole tester (GS38) and proving unit. Live tests only where unavoidable. Gas-side or boiler internals = Gas Safe engineer. Always follow the manufacturer's wiring diagram — wire colours and terminals vary by make.
